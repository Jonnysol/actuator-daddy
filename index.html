Scroll to take him apart. Everything here follows the Verros spec sheet except the gearbox, which is a secret, so ours is an illustration.

  1. Output flangeWhere the load goes.
  2. Front plate16 bolts. Same as the logo.

  4. RotorA ring of magnets that goes round when told.
  5. MotorDriven further than a motor this size has gone before. That glow is copper working.
  6. DriverHis own, built into the housing. Power and comms in, motion out.
  7. HousingFinned, anodized, built by hand in San Francisco.
